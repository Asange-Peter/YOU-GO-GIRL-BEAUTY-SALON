Our Beauty Services

Discover our range of beauty services for everyday self-care, protective styling, special occasions and complete glam looks. If you are unsure which service suits your needs, contact us and describe the look you have in mind.

Hair & Protective Styling

Hair Wash: A refreshing cleansing service that prepares your hair for styling and helps you maintain a clean, refreshed look.

Knotless Braids: A popular protective style that provides a neat, versatile braided finish for everyday wear and special occasions.

Boho Braids: A relaxed, textured braided style inspired by bohemian looks, with added curls and movement.

Lash Extensions

Lash extensions are designed to enhance the eyes and add definition to your overall beauty look. They can be incorporated into a natural beauty routine or paired with a glam makeup look for an event.

  • Discuss your preferred lash appearance before the service.
  • Consider the type of look you want to achieve.
  • Follow the aftercare information provided after your appointment.

Makeup Services

Natural Glam: A soft, polished makeup style that enhances your features without creating an overly dramatic finish.

Full Glam: A more defined makeup look for celebrations, events, photographs and occasions where you want a statement finish.

When enquiring, tell us what type of event you are attending and whether you prefer a natural, soft or more dramatic finish.

Wigs & Hair Products

Wigs can provide a flexible way to change your hairstyle and explore different looks. We also provide hair product options to support your beauty routine between salon visits.

Product and wig availability can change, so please contact the salon for current options, sizes, styles and pricing.

Preparing for Your Appointment

  1. Choose the service you would like.
  2. Save a reference image if you have a specific style in mind.
  3. Ask whether your chosen service requires hair, extensions or other products.
  4. Confirm the appointment date, time and price.
  5. Arrive prepared and communicate any preferences before the service begins.
