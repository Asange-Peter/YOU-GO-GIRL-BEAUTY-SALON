About us and our team

Levels on ice is a beauty salon that was established the year before covid 19 (2019). This business started as a very small local salon where people could only braid and wash their hair only and over the years it expanded into a huge business where people can now do their hair, nails, lashes, makeup and also sell wigs to customers.
