What Can Change the Final Price?

  • Hair Length: longer hair may require additional time and materials.
  • Hair Density: thicker hair may require additional styling time.
  • Style Choice: detailed or customised styles can require more work.
  • Extensions: some styles may require additional hair or accessories.
  • Products: specialised products or additional materials may affect the total.
